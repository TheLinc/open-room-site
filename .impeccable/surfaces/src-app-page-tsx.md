# Product page (src/app/page.tsx)

Mode: Persuade. One route, the whole site.

## Brief

- Audience: a developer who already runs Claude Code in a terminal, arriving from the README or a social post, solution-aware and wary of anything with a microphone. Within one viewport they must know what Open Room is, believe it is safe, and see the download.
- Primary action: Download for Windows via the GitHub releases redirect, in the first viewport and repeated at the close. macOS named as coming, no button. Secondary: view the source.
- Proof on hand: the mechanism demonstrated on the page, the GitHub repo and live star count, the licence. No testimonials or metrics; none invented. All product imagery is drawn in code from the app's real screens (reference captures in assets/app-reference); only the hero carries a caption; element crops were tried and rejected because they carry the app's white surface onto the page.
- Sections in order: hero; how it works (three distinct demonstrations, not three identical cards); feature tour with the crew as cast, a pinned visual beside a scrolling list; free and open source with repo and stars; safety as a full-bleed dark band; get started (install, sign in with your Claude Code login, name your first agent); FAQ answering API key, what leaves the machine, open mic, quota; footer with GitHub.
- Copy: rewritten for a product page in the company-brain voice; waitlist form dropped from the page, plumbing kept.
- Reference qualities to achieve: characters distributed down the page in margins and on panels, each with a job (Strawberry); sections that pin while their idea changes, motion tied to reading (Calendly); speech as a visible object and graphics that break the margins (Wispr); a minimal pixel world behind the hero with the crew in it (Cofounder, Pixel Blast).
- Anti-goals: no human presenter, no fabricated claims, no retro-game chrome, no pixel typefaces, no cream-and-serif, no kicker labels above headings, no same-size icon cards as page structure, no hard offset shadows.
- States: the hero script plays once and rests. Reduced motion shows the finished state, a still desk row, a quiet field. Download handles Windows only. FAQ opens one item at a time.
- Open: exact installer asset name on the latest release; whether the app's own theme is light or dark (page is light for now).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your agents are a team you talk to, and this page shows it happening: a clean product surface in the app's own register, with five pixel characters as the only place pixels live. It refuses the category's centred-headline-over-screenshot arrangement as a static picture: the screenshot here is a working scene, the crew sits on top of it, and your voice visibly reaches them.

OWN-WORLD: Zinc neutrals on a light ground, Geist for everything, Geist Mono only for paths, keys and captions. Hairline borders, 8 to 14px radii, one filled black button, hover as a shade change. Colour is the five agent hues, used as accents and never as fills behind text. Pixel art appears in three forms only: the crew's faces, the crew at their desks, and one signature element, the voice pill in the headline. Behind the hero, Pixel Blast: a lattice of teal pixel squares, lifted toward the ground, faded at the edges. With all copy removed the page is recognisable by the black button, the pixel crew on a white window, and the pixel field.

STORY: The visitor reads the headline and sees, without scrolling, a line being spoken into the app, an agent's screen lighting at its desk, and a reply coming back. They understand that one voice reaches many named agents, believe nothing leaves their machine because the page says so plainly and the app's permission model is shown, and download.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header: wordmark left, anchors, a star-count chip, the black Download for Windows button right. Centred headline at 64px, weight 500, tracking -0.035em, "Talk" set in white inside the voice pill with the level bars after it, sized to the cap height. Lead below in muted zinc at 19px. Two buttons: Download for Windows filled, View the source outlined. One mono line under them: macOS soon, nothing leaves your machine. Then the app window, 1040px wide, with the five agents at their desks perched on its top edge, and inside it the agent rail with faces, the thread, and the mic bar. A mono caption beneath names it an illustration. The pixel field sits behind the copy and fades before the window.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: One voice, many desks. When a line is said, the pill's bars rise and take the named agent's colour; the line types into the window's thread with the wake word in that colour; that agent's row lights, and 700ms later it glances over its shoulder at its desk and its screen lights. The window's script plays once, about twenty-five seconds, then rests. The desks type throughout in stepped two-frame motion, each agent on its own offset; a done agent rests its hands. Behind the copy sits Pixel Blast, a slowly drifting lattice of teal pixel squares with edge fade, loaded after the page is interactive and answering only to the pointer; it is texture, not part of the story. Interface motion is smooth; character motion is stepped; the two never mix.

FORM: The standing exit, the category standard executed at full craft, sitting alongside Cursor, Conductor and Linear. Chosen by the owner over the roll's Switchboard and the pick's Party Screen after a mock of each. Seed key c4b1c708, kind canon.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
